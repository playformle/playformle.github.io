How to Play: Linked Up
1

Find the groups

16 words are hidden on the board. They secretly belong to 4 groups of 4. Your job is to find each group.

2

Select 4 words

Click four words you think belong together, then hit Submit. If you're right, the group is revealed in colour!

VENUS

4 mistakes allowed

You can get it wrong up to 4 times. Watch the dots — each wrong guess uses one up.

How to Play: Word Web
1

Find words in the grid

A 6×8 grid of letters hides several theme words. Click letters to select them — they must be connected (touching in any direction).

2

Follow the theme

All hidden words relate to the theme shown at the top. Find them all to complete the puzzle!

3

Find the Spangram

One special word — the Spangram — connects two opposite edges of the grid. It perfectly captures the theme!
